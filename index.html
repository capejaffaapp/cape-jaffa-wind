Methodology and limitations

This prototype retrieves live model forecasts from Open-Meteo. Where available, it combines ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS and ICON wind vectors with equal weights. It estimates gusts from the available models and displays model disagreement; this is not a calibrated probability or verified confidence score. Model grids may not resolve coastal shelter, local topography or rapid squalls. A historical skill-weighted system requires a backend that regularly archives forecasts and Bureau of Meteorology observations. BOM Cape Jaffa station 026095 is a verified observation source, but live BOM readings are not yet integrated into this prototype. Check official BOM marine forecasts and warnings before boating.

Forecast and marine data: Open-Meteo and its contributing providers. Marine model predictions are not direct observations. API access may be subject to non-commercial licensing and service limits.
